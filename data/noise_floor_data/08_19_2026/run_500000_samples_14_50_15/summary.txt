 NOISE FLOOR  —  the noise each photodiode adds on its own
====================================================================
Extra electronic/pickup noise per channel, measured only where no
carrier or interference lives, then scaled to the analysis band.
Record : 16000 samples (3 s)   Fs = 5000 Hz   carrier = 930.5 Hz
Scaled : to the 4 Hz demod window (same band the cross-sensor cell uses)

  sensor     carrier mV noise uV rms   SNR dB    Var(n) mV^2  vs quietest  consistency
  ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
  A2V-16_A1      28.402        100.5     46.0       1.01e-02         1.8x      varies*
  A2V-16_A2      29.690        118.7     45.0       1.41e-02         2.5x      varies*
  A2V-16_A3      20.089        119.6     41.5       1.43e-02         2.5x   consistent
  A2V-16_A4      32.180         75.8     49.6       5.74e-03         1.0x      varies*

How to read this
  carrier mV    carrier amplitude a_i, from integrating the PSD peak at the
                carrier (tone power = a_i^2/2). This is the wanted signal.
  noise uV rms  RMS of the channel's own noise over the demod band, in µV.
                It is sqrt(Var(n)) — a standard deviation (zero-mean), not a
                peak and not a per-√Hz density.
  SNR dB        carrier power over noise power in the demod band,
                10·log10((a_i^2/2)/Var(n)) — i.e. carrier-RMS / noise-RMS in dB.
                Per-channel carrier SNR, not the inter-diode PUF comparison.
  Var(n) mV^2   the same noise as a variance — it IS the Var(n_i) the
                cross-sensor cell prints for this diode.
  vs quietest   how many times noisier than the best channel.
  consistency   'consistent' = same floor everywhere; 'varies*' = the floor
                changes with frequency (usually a 1/f rise down low), so read
                the number as a typical mid-band value, not an exact constant.

Where each number came from
  A2V-16_A1 averaged over 78 clean bands (e.g. 42, 27, 874 Hz);  28 rejected as too near a spur
  A2V-16_A2 averaged over 80 clean bands (e.g. 1755, 1944, 27 Hz);  26 rejected as too near a spur
  A2V-16_A3 averaged over 83 clean bands (e.g. 27, 1755, 491 Hz);  23 rejected as too near a spur
  A2V-16_A4 averaged over 81 clean bands (e.g. 195, 27, 206 Hz);  25 rejected as too near a spur
